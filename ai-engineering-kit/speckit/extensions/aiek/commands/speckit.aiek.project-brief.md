---
description: "Shape a new project's BRD into a Project Brief without planning"
---

## Input

```text
$ARGUMENTS
```

Read the BRD at the supplied project-relative path. Use the installed `shaping-project`
skill and its template to produce `docs/briefs/project-brief.md`. Stop after the Brief;
the roadmap belongs to the next Spec Kit command. If a material product choice remains,
ask the owner and mark the brief pending rather than guessing.

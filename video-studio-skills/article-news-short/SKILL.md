# Article / news short

Read `video-studio` first and follow its chat versus managed-run routing. Read [production](../references/production.md) and [artifacts](../references/artifacts.md).

Default: about 60s, Vietnamese cloned OmniVoice narration, translated English `caption-highlight`, portrait HyperFrames. Extend duration only when the brief explicitly allows it; do not compress speech to force a duration.

Read the supplied content. For factual claims, retain the original URL, publication time when available, event time when different, and the exact supporting passage or data point. For breaking news, cross-check the primary announcement. Label opinions from a personal note as opinions. Avoid “just launched”, “currently trending” and similar claims without dated evidence.

If access or transcript extraction fails, report what source is missing and request the needed text. Do not summarize a title as if the full article was read.

Choose one useful insight instead of squeezing a full article into a list. Write naturally, with a concrete opening and grounded ending. Remove AI stock phrases, rhetorical contrasts, repetitive triads and generic hype. No invented first-person experiences.

Use screenshots of the actual source and relevant visual evidence. Keep explanatory overlays short. Translate captions after the Vietnamese narration is settled; align them to Vietnamese sentence/phrase timings rather than pretending English words match Vietnamese word timestamps.

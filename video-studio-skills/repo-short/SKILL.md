# Repo short

Read `video-studio` first; follow its chat versus managed-run routing. Read [production](../references/production.md) and [artifacts](../references/artifacts.md).

Default: 45–60s, portrait, minimalist editorial visuals, Vietnamese cloned OmniVoice narration, English `caption-emoji-pop`, HyperFrames. The brief can override these choices. Do not switch runtime/provider silently.

Research the repo README, official docs and release history. Distinguish documented capability, a demonstrated action, and personal interpretation. Do not invent hands-on experience, popularity, launch dates, savings or benchmarks. A social post/TiniX listing is discovery evidence, not sufficient proof of a technical claim.

Pick one grounded angle: a concrete problem, how this repo handles it, and a useful limitation or deployment detail. Write an honest hook in the first 3 seconds. Use conversational Vietnamese, varied sentence lengths and specific examples. Finish with a grounded takeaway or a short natural follow CTA when appropriate.

Prefer real screenshots and recordings from the supplied repository/product. Never create fake product screens or say a demo was tested without evidence. Use motion graphics to explain mechanics, not as a replacement for missing evidence.

Keep clean narration, engine-specific TTS text and translated captions separate. Apply the pronunciation/sample gate in the shared production contract before batch audio.
